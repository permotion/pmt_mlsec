## Input features — `features_v2.parquet` (17 features)

Generated by `preprocess_csic_v2.py`. Inherits the 15 features from v1 and adds 2 new ones validated in the v1 notebook.

**Inherited from v1** — unchanged:

`method_is_get`, `method_is_post`, `method_is_put`, `url_length`, `url_has_pct27`, `url_has_pct3c`, `url_has_dashdash`, `url_has_script`, `url_has_select`, `content_length`, `content_has_pct27`, `content_has_pct3c`, `content_has_dashdash`, `content_has_script`, `content_has_select`

**New in v2:**

| Feature | How it is calculated | Correlation with label | Why it is added |
|---|---|---|---|
| `url_pct_density` | `count('%') / len(url)` | 0.267 | Gives context to `url_length` — a long URL with dense encoding is different from a long URL with normal text |
| `url_param_count` | `count('=')` in the URL | 0.146 | Attacks inject payloads into parameters — more `=` is signal of more parameters |

**Discarded in this cycle** (evaluated in v1, no signal):

| Feature | Reason |
|---|---|
| `url_has_traversal` | `../` never appears literal in the dataset — always percent-encoded as `%2F` |
| `post_has_pct27` | Insufficient GET∩POST intersection — NaN |

## What changed since v1

The two features validated in v1 were incorporated into the official preprocessing:

| New feature | What it measures | Why it helps |
|---|---|---|
| `url_pct_density` | Ratio of encoded chars (`%XX`) over total URL length | Gives context to `url_length` — a long URL with high encoding density is very different from a long URL with normal text |
| `url_param_count` | Amount of `=` in the URL | Attacks have more parameters to inject payloads |

`url_has_traversal` and `post_has_pct27` were discarded (NaN in v1 — no signal).

This notebook re-trains the **4 complete models** with `features_v2.parquet` to confirm the improvement with the official pipeline.

## Analysis

- **Consistent improvement across all models** — the increase is not by chance: all raise ROC-AUC between +0.006 and +0.012, Precision between +0.009 and +0.049.
- **FP reduction:** RF -382, LightGBM -371. There are ~380 legitimate requests that the model no longer classifies as an attack.
- **The ceiling rises** from ~0.94 to ~0.95 ROC-AUC — we haven't reached the information limit yet.
- **Remaining gap:** Max Precision 0.704 vs target 0.85 → 0.146 left to close.
- **LightGBM warning:** `X does not have valid feature names` — cosmetic, doesn't affect results.

## Decision

Continue iterating. The next hypothesis: there is untapped signal in the **content of POST requests**. The EDA showed POST attacks having 35% longer bodies, but `content_has_*` have low correlation because they are calculated over the whole dataset (including GETs with empty content).

Before v3, integrate **MLflow** for systematic tracking.
